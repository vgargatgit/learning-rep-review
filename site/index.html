Representation principles

Questions to ask before choosing a model

Architecture starts after the domain, information and transformation requirements are understood.

01

What is the object?

A scalar, category, sequence, set, image, graph or event stream carries different structural assumptions.

02

What must be preserved?

Aggregation, compression and preprocessing can silently discard recency, multiplicity, topology or rare events.

03

What should be invariant?

Order, translation, rotation or unit changes should be ignored only when they do not alter the task answer.

04

What geometry is real?

Integer category IDs, linear clock time and arbitrary distances can manufacture relationships that do not exist.

05

What does the objective reward?

The training game defines which similarities, distinctions and shortcuts become encoded.

06

Can it leak the future?

Every fitted transform, time window, target encoding and split belongs inside a clearly defined validation boundary.

The central habit

Do not ask only, “Which model should I use?”

Ask what the model is allowed to see, how that information is organised, which transformations matter and what the learning objective will make easy to encode.
